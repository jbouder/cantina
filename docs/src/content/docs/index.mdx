## What it does

<div class="features not-content">
  <Feature label="PEDALS">
    Play through a chain of stock pedals in real time: tuner, gate, compressor, three drives with a clean blend, EQ, modulation, an octaver and pitch shifter, delay, reverb, and an amp and cabinet built on impulse responses.
  </Feature>
  <Feature label="TRACKS">
    Record takes onto a canvas timeline, then move, trim and split them. The mixer sits in the track headers and every edit can be undone.
  </Feature>
  <Feature label="DRUMS">
    Program beats on a step grid with velocity and swing, played by a sampler over stock kits. Patterns go on the timeline as clips and follow the tempo map.
  </Feature>
  <Feature label="PLUGINS">
    Audio Units and CLAP plugins go in the same chain as the stock pedals. Their editors open in their own windows, and their latency is compensated across tracks.
  </Feature>
  <Feature label="DESIGNER">
    Build your own pedal as a graph of filters, waveshapers, delays and LFOs while it plays. It is saved as plain JSON you can share.
  </Feature>
  <Feature label="STAGE">
    Visualizer scenes react to what you play and lock to the tempo map's beats and bars. Performance mode puts the stage full screen with a pedalboard over it.
  </Feature>
</div>

## Six skins, one studio

The whole UI is drawn through semantic tokens, so a skin can change the shape, material and displays as well as the colour. This site is drawn in them too: Classic in the dark theme and Brushed metal in the light one. [Make your own](/guides/skins/).
